## Task description

1. Conducted the first substantive reconnaissance against the new AODN portal, which has replaced the platform used during the earlier audit sessions. The new portal organises holdings by voyage rather than by instrument sub-facility (SOOP-ASF, SOOP-BGC, SOOP-XBT, and similar), delivers data as CSV rather than NetCDF, and exposes a parameter-tag vocabulary that maps cleanly onto the six EAMP priority variables (SST, SSS, air temperature, atmospheric pressure, wind, pCO2).
2. Inspected 38 Nuyina results returned by the new portal under a vessel-name query, triaged them into three categories: 13 results downloaded immediately to the MacBook Downloads folder (216 MB of CSV across five seasons from 2021-22 through 2025-26), 5 results submitted for asynchronous preparation by AODN with download links to follow by email, and 20 results inspected but not downloaded as duplicates, off-scope content, or lower-priority records for the EAMP variables.
3. Transferred the 13 downloaded CSV files from the MacBook to Gadi under data/raw/ship/aodn_downloads/new_portal_nuyina/, preserving the project's established directory layout and naming convention.
4. Built the new-portal inventory module at src/eamp/ship/new_portal_inventory.py with three functions covering filename parsing for the voyage identifier (season and version), CSV inspection for row counts and column structure, and priority-variable matching against a configurable token dictionary. Produced the orchestrator script at scripts/run_new_portal_inventory.py to compose the three functions and write a two-sheet Excel inventory under data/processed/ship/.
5. Diagnosed and resolved a conda environment activation issue that caused ModuleNotFoundError on first run; the eamp environment had not been re-activated after a new session and the script was running against a user-level Python 3.9 rather than the conda Python 3.11. Re-activated the environment via the project's defensive helper, confirmed python-dotenv and openpyxl were already present in the conda environment, and re-ran the orchestrator successfully.
6. Produced the inventory artefact at data/processed/ship/eampB_aodn_new_portal_nuyina_inventory_2026-05-27.xlsx covering 13 CSV files, 520,949 total rows, 226 MB of underlying data, and five distinct operational seasons of RSV Nuyina.
7. Drafted the reconnaissance decision record at docs/decisions/2026-05-27-new-portal-reconnaissance-nuyina.md documenting the substantive differences between the old and new portals and the implications for the engagement's ingestion and analytical pipelines.
8. Committed and pushed all engineering work to the GitHub remote in a single coherent commit (fe67d4e); diagnosed and resolved a transient GitHub commit_refs error by retrying the push after a brief interval, with the second attempt succeeding cleanly without any local change.
